# Chart readings

All of this is common convention, written as tendencies, and **draft** until a Jyotish reviewer approves it (see
`docs/content-review.md`, generated by `python -m astrodaily.tools.export_review`).

- **Dasha text.** `/v1/kundli` and `/v1/profiles/{id}/kundli` give every main period and sub-period a `meaning`: a line on
  what the main period is like (`chart_meanings.json` → `dasha`) and, for a sub-period, a line put together from the two
  planets' themes.
- **Other charts.** `GET /v1/profiles/{id}/chart/{moon|navamsa|saptamsa|dashamsa}` gives the planets of that chart with their
  sign, house and the same planet-in-sign and planet-in-house text as the birth chart. The Moon chart counts houses from the
  Moon sign with the birth signs; the others use their own signs and their own Lagna. Needs a birth time (409 without).
- **Strength, aspects, yogas.** Each planet in the kundli has `strength` (a simple guide: sign and house points, less one when
  combust: strong, steady or tender) and `aspects` (the houses it looks at and the planets in them). `yogas` lists the
  yogas, doshas and the Sade Sati phase that are present (`calc/yogas.py`, wording in `content/yoga_text.py`). The rules are
  listed in the review document.
- **Matching.** `GET /v1/me/match/{profile_id}?swap=true` puts the other person in the first column of the tables.
  `GET /v1/me/match/{profile_id}/summary?lang=&swap=` is a short model-written summary of the match, built once per ordered
  pair and language and kept in `match_summaries` (cleared when either person's birth details change; in the export). No
  names or birth details go to the model.
