# Brief for a Jyotish reviewer (draft)

Use this to find and brief one person, ideally a practising Jyotishi who reads Hindi.

## What AstroDaily is
A daily guidance app for India. Each user gets a short card: what suits the day, what to go easy on, and
a good time window. Everything is computed from the sky (Lahiri sidereal, Moon sign or birth chart). No
predictions of illness, loss or death; the tone is calm and practical.

## What we need from the reviewer (about 4 to 6 hours for round one)
1. **Rule tables** (13 items) in `docs/rules-review-checklist.md`: mark each OK, CHANGE TO ..., or DROP.
   Most valuable: Gochara houses for Rahu and Ketu, the Tara Bala split, nakshatra categories, the nine
   inauspicious yogas, and whether to apply vedha.
2. **Sample wording** in `docs/content-draft-v0.md`: is the meaning right, is the tone acceptable, is
   anything you would never say to a user. Fix the Hindi.
3. **Two judgement calls**: which tradition variant the app should follow (for example Choghadiya as a
   regional option), and whether we may use the word "classical" anywhere (current answer: no).

## What they do not need to do
No coding, no accounts, no scoring weights (the app's own design). A shared document with comments is
enough.

## Where to find one (suggestions)
- A Jyotish teacher or panchang author reached through a college Sanskrit or Jyotir-vigyan department.
- A practising Jyotishi from a community you trust, paid per round, with a fixed scope.
- Offer: fixed fee per round, named credit in the app if they want it.

## Fee and terms to settle first
- Fee per round and number of rounds.
- Written agreement that wording they approve is ours to use (the prose is original; the rules are method).
- They review rules and wording only; they do not endorse accuracy claims, and the app makes none.

## Open questions we will ask them
See the "Question for the reviewer" column in the checklist.
